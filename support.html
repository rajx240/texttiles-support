TextTiles

Support

TextTiles keeps reusable text on Mac, iPhone, and iPad. On iPhone and iPad, Notes holds original writing and Canvas holds personal boards.

Before requesting help

  • Confirm you are running the latest App Store version.
  • On Mac, choose Export Snippets… before troubleshooting local data.
  • On iPhone or iPad, use Library & Settings → Backup & Restore → Save Library Backup for tiles. Use the Notes or Canvas options menu to export those separate backups too.
  • For sync issues, confirm every device uses the intended Apple Account and iCloud is available. Allow pending changes to finish before signing out or switching accounts. Notes and Canvas may ask you to review a changed account before resuming sync.
  • Include your platform, OS version, TextTiles version/build, and reproduction steps. Do not post private snippet contents.

Contact support

Open a support request. A GitHub account is required to post in this public issue queue. Do not include passwords, personal text, board pictures, or other sensitive data.

Set up the TextTiles keyboard

In TextTiles, open Library & Settings → TextTiles Keyboard. In iOS Settings, choose General → Keyboard → Keyboards → Add New Keyboard → TextTiles. Full Access is not required. Return to the app's Try It Here field, hold the globe key, and select TextTiles.

Tap a tile to insert its saved text. Choose which tiles appear in Keyboard Tiles. After editing or syncing in the main app, reopen the keyboard to refresh. Secure fields and apps that prohibit custom keyboards use the system keyboard instead.

Where to find your writing

Notes stay separate from Library tiles until you deliberately save wording as a tile. Canvas keeps independent snapshots of selected notes and tiles, plus board-owned words and pictures. Deleting a board does not delete its source note or tile. Deleted Notes and boards appear in their own Recently Deleted views for 30 days. Delete Library Everywhere clears Library tiles; it does not clear Notes or Canvas. Read the Privacy Policy for retention and export details.
