Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 23, 2026

TextTiles stores the text tiles you create locally on your device and, when iCloud is available, synchronizes them through your private Apple CloudKit database. The TextTiles developer does not collect, sell, rent, or share that content.

Data handling

  • TextTiles has no separate account system. iCloud sync uses the Apple Account already signed in on the device and remains optional.
  • No analytics, advertising, tracking, or third-party SDK collects data.
  • Apple iCloud receives encrypted-in-transit tile records for private-database synchronization. The user owns that private database, its content is not visible to the TextTiles developer in CloudKit Console, and no TextTiles-operated server receives it.
  • On macOS, TextTiles reads the current clipboard only when you choose Use Copied Text.
  • On macOS and iOS/iPadOS, TextTiles writes to the clipboard only after you explicitly copy a saved tile.
  • JSON import and export access only files you select through the system file picker.
  • Local backup files remain in TextTiles’ Application Support directory on each device.
  • CloudKit engine metadata, an iCloud account marker, and content-free deletion tombstones remain outside exported JSON backups.

Retention and deletion

Your local tiles remain until you delete them in the app or remove the app and its local data. Synced tile content remains in your private iCloud database and on connected devices until deletion synchronizes. Content-free tombstones may remain so an offline device cannot restore deleted text. Removing the app from one device does not by itself delete its iCloud data. Exported JSON files remain wherever you choose to save them and must be deleted separately.

To delete the complete synced library, open Settings → iCloud Sync → Delete Synced Library on Mac, or open Library & Settings → Data Management and choose Delete Library Everywhere on iPhone or iPad. After you confirm Delete Everywhere, TextTiles deletes its private CloudKit zone, the local library, automatic backups, preserved corrupt copies, and content-bearing sync state. Other synced devices clear their local copies when they reconnect. A content-free deletion generation, the associated Apple Account record identifier, and its timestamp remain locally and in the private CloudKit database so offline devices cannot restore deleted text. This action cannot delete JSON files you previously exported outside TextTiles. If local or iCloud cleanup is temporarily unavailable after deletion commits, TextTiles keeps uploads blocked and retries the remaining cleanup automatically.

If Apple reports that the TextTiles CloudKit zone was purged or its encrypted data was reset, TextTiles clears the synced local library, automatic backups, preserved corrupt copies, and cached sync state on that device. This prevents a later recovery from restoring text removed through iCloud.

Changes

If TextTiles adds an online service or changes its data practices, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosure will be updated before that change ships.
