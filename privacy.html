TextTiles

Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 28, 2026

TextTiles stores the text tiles and, on iPhone and iPad, the notes you create locally on your device. When iCloud is available, it synchronizes them through separate areas of your private Apple CloudKit database. The TextTiles developer does not collect, sell, rent, or share that content.

Data handling

  • TextTiles has no separate account system. iCloud sync uses the Apple Account already signed in on the device and remains optional.
  • No analytics, advertising, tracking, or third-party SDK collects data.
  • Apple iCloud receives encrypted-in-transit tile and note records for private-database synchronization. The user owns that private database, its content is not visible to the TextTiles developer in CloudKit Console, and no TextTiles-operated server receives it.
  • On macOS, TextTiles reads the current clipboard only when you choose Use Copied Text.
  • On macOS and iOS/iPadOS, TextTiles writes to the clipboard only after you explicitly copy a saved tile.
  • JSON import and export access only files you select through the system file picker.
  • Local backup files remain in TextTiles’ Application Support directory on each device.
  • CloudKit engine metadata, an iCloud account marker, and content-free deletion tombstones remain outside exported JSON backups.

TextTiles keyboard on iPhone and iPad

The optional keyboard works without Full Access. The main app writes a local copy of included tiles and appearance preferences to a shared App Group container. The keyboard reads this copy to display and insert your saved text; it does not perform iCloud sync or send typed text over the network. Open TextTiles to receive synced changes, then reopen the keyboard to refresh its local view.

All tiles are included by default. Choose which tiles appear under Library & Settings → TextTiles Keyboard → Keyboard Tiles. Excluding a tile removes it from this device's keyboard without deleting it from the library. Hiding a preview conceals the tile body on screen; tapping the tile still inserts its complete text into the active app.

The keyboard transiently reads surrounding text supplied by iOS to provide capitalization and double-space punctuation. This context is not logged, saved, or transmitted by TextTiles. Tap timing is used locally to recognize double-space and Shift gestures. Text you deliberately insert is handled by the destination app under that app's own privacy practices.

Notes on iPhone and iPad

Notes hold your original plain-text writing separately from Library tiles and Create drafts. They are saved locally and synchronize through a separate area of your private iCloud database. Notes do not enter Library search, Compose, or the TextTiles keyboard unless you explicitly copy wording into a tile and save that tile. You can export or import a portable Notes JSON file using the system file picker; exported files remain wherever you choose to save them.

Notes keep local safety copies and a recovery journal for interrupted edits. If a damaged file needs recovery, Notes pauses iCloud synchronization until you review the recovered writing. Changing Apple Accounts also pauses Notes sync until you choose whether to use the new account. Neither action moves Notes into another account automatically.

Retention and deletion

Your local tiles remain until you delete them in the app or remove the app and its local data. Synced tile content remains in your private iCloud database and on connected devices until deletion synchronizes. Content-free tombstones may remain so an offline device cannot restore deleted text. Removing the app from one device does not by itself delete its iCloud data. Exported JSON files remain wherever you choose to save them and must be deleted separately.

Deleting a note moves it to Recently Deleted for 30 days, when you can restore it. After that window, TextTiles removes its title and body from local Notes storage and syncs a content-free deletion marker to prevent an offline copy from silently restoring it. A separately exported Notes file remains yours to remove. Delete Synced Library affects Library tiles and its CloudKit area; it does not delete Notes or the Notes iCloud area.

To delete the complete synced library, open Settings → iCloud Sync → Delete Synced Library on Mac, or open Library & Settings → Data Management → Delete Library Everywhere on iPhone or iPad. After you confirm Delete Everywhere, TextTiles deletes its private CloudKit zone, the local library, automatic backups, preserved corrupt copies, and content-bearing sync state. Other synced devices clear their local copies when they reconnect. A content-free deletion generation, the associated Apple Account record identifier, and its timestamp remain locally and in the private CloudKit database so offline devices cannot restore deleted text. This action cannot delete JSON files you previously exported outside TextTiles. If local or iCloud cleanup is temporarily unavailable after deletion commits, TextTiles keeps uploads blocked and retries the remaining cleanup automatically.

If Apple reports that the TextTiles CloudKit zone was purged or its encrypted data was reset, TextTiles clears the synced local library, automatic backups, preserved corrupt copies, and cached sync state on that device. This prevents a later recovery from restoring text removed through iCloud.

Changes

If TextTiles adds an online service or changes its data practices, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosure will be updated before that change ships.
