TextTiles

Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 28, 2026

TextTiles stores the text tiles and, on iPhone and iPad, the notes and boards you create locally on your device. When iCloud is available, it synchronizes them through separate areas of your private Apple CloudKit database. The TextTiles developer does not collect, sell, rent, or share that content.

Data handling

  • TextTiles has no separate account system. iCloud sync uses the Apple Account already signed in on the device and remains optional.
  • No analytics, advertising, tracking, or third-party SDK collects data.
  • Apple iCloud receives encrypted-in-transit tile, note, board, and selected board-image records for private-database synchronization. The user owns that private database, its content is not visible to the TextTiles developer in CloudKit Console, and no TextTiles-operated server receives it.
  • On macOS, TextTiles reads the current clipboard only when you choose Use Copied Text.
  • On macOS and iOS/iPadOS, TextTiles writes to the clipboard only after an explicit copy action, including copying a tile, Create candidate, Note, or composed message.
  • JSON import and export access only files you select through the system file picker.
  • Local backup files remain in TextTiles’ Application Support directory on each device.
  • CloudKit engine metadata, an iCloud account marker, and content-free deletion tombstones remain outside exported JSON backups.

TextTiles keyboard on iPhone and iPad

The optional keyboard works without Full Access. The main app writes a local copy of included tiles and appearance preferences to a shared App Group container. The keyboard reads this copy to display and insert your saved text; it does not perform iCloud sync or send typed text over the network. Open TextTiles to receive synced changes, then reopen the keyboard to refresh its local view.

All tiles are included by default. Choose which tiles appear under Library & Settings → TextTiles Keyboard → Keyboard Tiles. Excluding a tile removes it from this device's keyboard without deleting it from the library. Hiding a preview conceals the tile body on screen; tapping the tile still inserts its complete text into the active app.

The keyboard transiently reads surrounding text supplied by iOS to provide capitalization and double-space punctuation. This context is not logged, saved, or transmitted by TextTiles. Tap timing is used locally to recognize double-space and Shift gestures. Text you deliberately insert is handled by the destination app under that app's own privacy practices.

Create writing assistance on iPhone and iPad

Create uses Apple's on-device Foundation Models for optional English text assistance on eligible devices with iOS 26 or 27 and Apple Intelligence enabled. There is no cloud-model fallback or TextTiles-operated server. Manual tile writing remains available when assistance is unavailable. Create accepts typed, pasted, or system-dictated text; its current screen does not offer image-to-text capture. TextTiles does not record audio or request microphone or speech-recognition permission. System keyboard dictation follows your device settings and may use Apple services.

Unsaved thoughts, candidates, and revision inputs stay in memory by default. Choosing Keep draft on this device stores one protected local checkpoint outside Library, App Group, exports, keyboard snapshots, and CloudKit; it is excluded from backup. The latest edit may be lost after an abrupt interruption. Removing the kept draft or beginning a new thought clears that checkpoint. Only an explicit Add or Save changes action puts accepted writing into Library, where normal sync and keyboard inclusion rules apply. Opening support does not attach your writing.

Notes on iPhone and iPad

Notes hold your original plain-text writing separately from Library tiles and Create drafts. They are saved locally and synchronize through a separate area of your private iCloud database. Notes do not enter Library search, Compose, or the TextTiles keyboard unless you explicitly copy wording into a tile and save that tile. You can export or import a portable Notes JSON file using the system file picker; exported files remain wherever you choose to save them.

Notes keep local safety copies and a recovery journal for interrupted edits. If a damaged file needs recovery, Notes pauses iCloud synchronization until you review the recovered writing. Changing Apple Accounts also pauses Notes sync until you choose whether to use the new account. Neither action moves Notes into another account automatically.

Canvas on iPhone and iPad

Canvas keeps personal boards separately from Library tiles and Notes. A board can hold your own text, snapshots of selected tile or Note passages, and pictures you choose through the system photo picker. A snapshot does not change its source, and deleting a board does not delete a source tile or Note. The photo picker may retrieve a selected iCloud photo before import. Canvas keeps board pictures locally and synchronizes boards and their picture assets through a separate area of your private iCloud database when available.

A restorable Canvas JSON backup includes board-owned writing and pictures. It remains wherever you save it and must be deleted separately. A shared board image or PDF is a separate export, not a restorable backup. Canvas may keep a local last-good backup and damaged files for recovery review. If storage recovery or the Apple Account needs review, Canvas pauses syncing rather than silently moving boards to a new account.

Retention and deletion

Your local tiles remain until you delete them in the app or remove the app and its local data. Synced tile content remains in your private iCloud database and on connected devices until deletion synchronizes. Content-free tombstones may remain so an offline device cannot restore deleted text. Removing the app from one device does not by itself delete its iCloud data. Exported JSON files remain wherever you choose to save them and must be deleted separately.

Deleting a note moves it to Recently Deleted for 30 days, when you can restore it. After that window, the app removes its title and body when it next opens or syncs and sends a content-free deletion marker to prevent an offline copy from silently restoring it. A separately exported Notes file remains yours to remove.

Deleting a Canvas board moves it to Recently Deleted for 30 days, with its writing and pictures available for restoration. After that window, the app replaces the board in active storage and its last-good backup with a content-free deletion marker when it next opens or syncs, and removes local picture files no longer referenced by another board. The marker prevents an offline copy from silently restoring the board. Preserved damaged recovery files may still hold older content because the app cannot safely decode them; they remain on this device for manual recovery until its app data is removed. Previously exported Canvas backups and shared images or PDFs remain wherever you saved them. The board record becomes a content-free marker in private iCloud when sync completes, but orphaned Canvas picture records can remain indefinitely in private iCloud storage; this version does not automatically remove them. Delete Synced Library affects Library tiles and its CloudKit area; it does not delete Notes or Canvas or their separate iCloud areas.

To delete the complete synced library, open Settings → iCloud Sync → Delete Synced Library on Mac, or open Library & Settings → Data Management → Delete Library Everywhere on iPhone or iPad. After you confirm Delete Everywhere, TextTiles deletes its private CloudKit zone, the local library, automatic backups, preserved corrupt copies, and content-bearing sync state. Other synced devices clear their local copies when they reconnect. A content-free deletion generation, the associated Apple Account record identifier, and its timestamp remain locally and in the private CloudKit database so offline devices cannot restore deleted text. This action cannot delete JSON files you previously exported outside TextTiles. If local or iCloud cleanup is temporarily unavailable after deletion commits, TextTiles keeps uploads blocked and retries the remaining cleanup automatically.

If Apple reports that the TextTiles CloudKit zone was purged or its encrypted data was reset, TextTiles clears the synced local library, automatic backups, preserved corrupt copies, and cached sync state on that device. This prevents a later recovery from restoring text removed through iCloud.

Changes
