Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 10, 2026

TextTiles stores the text tiles you create locally on your device. The app does not collect, transmit, sell, rent, or share personal data.

Data handling

  • No account is required.
  • No analytics, advertising, tracking, or third-party SDK collects data.
  • No cloud or network service receives your saved text.
  • On macOS, TextTiles reads the current clipboard only when you choose Use Copied Text.
  • TextTiles writes to the clipboard only after you explicitly copy a saved tile.
  • JSON import and export access only files you select through the system file picker.
  • Local backup files remain in TextTiles’ Application Support directory on Mac.

Retention and deletion

Your tiles remain on your device until you delete them in the app or remove the app and its local data. Exported JSON files remain wherever you save them and must be deleted separately. Because TextTiles does not collect or receive your data, there is no server-side account or data record to delete.

Changes

If TextTiles adds an online service or changes its data practices, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosure will be updated before that change ships.

Contact

Privacy questions can be submitted through the TextTiles support request form.
